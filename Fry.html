Fry CHEF

fry Chef (Saucier) – Focuses on sautéed dishes and sauces, ensuring flavors are balanced and textures are perfect. They handle pan-frying and mastering complex sauces, making them key players in fine dining kitchens.
What a Fry Chef Does:
Business & Customer Service Skills – If you plan to run a bakery, understanding marketing, budgeting, and customer satisfaction is essential.
