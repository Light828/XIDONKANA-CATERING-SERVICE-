Vegetarian CHEF

Specializes in preparing delicious, plant-based dishes using vegetables, fruits, grains, legumes, nuts, and dairy alternatives. Creativity is key in crafting flavorful, visually appealing meals without meat.
What a VEGETARIAN Chef Does:
Business & Customer Service Skills – If you plan to run a bakery, understanding marketing, budgeting, and customer satisfaction is essential. Pairing with Side Dishes: Matching seafood with appropriate sides like rice, vegetables, or pasta.

Essential Skills for a VEGETARIAN Chef

  • Knife Skills: Precision cutting, chopping, and slicing for efficiency and presentation.
  • Food Safety & Hygiene: Understanding sanitation, storage, and kitchen safety.
